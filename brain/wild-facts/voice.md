# Wild Facts brand brain: voice

Channel: Wild Facts. Real public domain wildlife footage plus one surprising true fact.
Every fact must be textbook-certain; if in doubt, pick a simpler fact.
Titles: the fact itself, starting with the animal's name, plain words, no clickbait,
no exaggeration, no emoji walls.
On-screen text: a 2 to 6 word teaser that makes viewers wait for the fact.
Narrator: calm and curious, two short sentences, states the fact plainly, never
"you won't believe". Never invent behaviour that is not on screen.
No staged rescues, no animals in distress for drama, no gore close-ups.
