# Celeste Arden: character bible (approved Oct 26, 2026; face reference: balcony portrait)

## Who she is
Celeste Arden is an AI-generated luxury lifestyle persona, clearly an adult (late twenties).
She lives the soft-life fantasy: slow mornings in beautiful hotels, long lunches by the sea,
private dinners, quiet first-class travel. She is warm, a little playful, self-aware about how
over the top her life is, and never mean about anyone else's. The appeal is the place, the
light and the mood, with her as the friendly guide. She is openly AI and says so in her bio.

## Look (keep identical in every shot)
- Face: oval face, warm olive skin, soft dark brown eyes, straight dark brows, a small
  beauty mark under the left eye, natural makeup with a bronze lip.
- Hair: long dark chestnut hair, soft loose waves, usually down; sleek low bun for dinners.
- Build: slim, average height; posture relaxed and confident.
- Signature details: thin gold hoop earrings, a plain gold watch on the left wrist,
  a cream silk scarf that shows up often.
- Wardrobe: quiet luxury. Linen sets, tailored trousers, cashmere knits, long silk slip
  dresses for evening, trench coats, loafers and flat sandals. Neutral palette (cream, camel,
  olive, black, white) with one colour accent per outfit. Resort wear at pools and beaches,
  shown in wide or medium shots as part of the scene, never posed for the body.
- Style: photoreal, cinematic, 35mm film look, golden-hour or soft window light, shallow
  depth of field, handheld phone-POV feel for "day in the life" shots.

## How she "talks"
She does not speak on camera. On-screen text carries the story in her voice, short and
first person ("POV: you finally booked the sea-view room"). Soundtrack is music plus the
natural ambience of the place (waves, cutlery, rain on a window).

## World
Rotating destinations inspired by the Amalfi coast, the French Riviera, Lake Como, the
Swiss Alps, Santorini, Dubai, Kyoto, the Maldives and Paris. Places are never named as a
real hotel, restaurant or brand; no visible logos anywhere. Recurring touches: her morning
espresso on a balcony, a hardback book she never finishes, a small white dog named Biscuit
who appears now and then.

## Episode rules
- 15 to 25 seconds, 3 to 5 shots, one clear mood or mini story (arrival, morning routine,
  getting ready for dinner, a room tour, "rate this view", a rainy day in).
- The hook is in the first second: a striking view or a reveal, plus 3 to 7 words of text.
- Vary the destination, time of day and series every episode.
- Never sexual: no lingerie, no bedroom poses, no body-focused framing, no innuendo in
  text or captions. Glamour comes from the setting, wardrobe and light.
- Never a real person's likeness, never real brand logos, never claims she is human.
- Every post is labelled as AI-generated.
