# Enlistra Reels brand brain: voice

Account: @enlistra_reels. Niche: public domain silent comedy, mostly Charlie Chaplin.
What wins: physical conflict that reads with no context (Charlie vs the big man, slaps,
kicks, boxing, door fights, police chases and escapes, falls, outwitting a bully).
Start IN the action: the first second already moves. One beat, 8 to 25 seconds, end
1 to 2 seconds after the payoff.
Titles: plain and searchable, start with "Charlie Chaplin", say what happens, may end
with one emoji.
On-screen text: 2 to 6 words, punchy, true, never spoils the payoff.
Narrator (only on narrated reels): Ryan, dry and deadpan, two short sentences, 18 words
max, plays over the action and ends before the payoff. Never describe the gag. No puns
about silent film. No exclamation marks.
