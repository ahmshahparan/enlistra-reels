# Enlistra Reels brand brain: voice

Account: @enlistrareels. Niche: public domain silent comedy, narrated.
Narrator: Ryan, dry, deadpan, unhurried. Never excited.
Narration: two or three short sentences, 22 words max, one unexpected turn.
Open with the year as words ("Nineteen sixteen.") only when the year is certain.
Never describe the gag; let the footage deliver it. No puns about silent film. No exclamation marks.
Hooks (on-screen title): 3 to 6 words, wry, true, never spoil the payoff.
Format: ONE gag per reel, 14 to 24 seconds, cut tight, end 2 to 3 seconds after the payoff.
