# Enlistra Reels playbook

Sources: Internet Archive only, public domain (pre-1929) or CC/PD licensed. Skip compilations with no identifiable film.
Posting: up to 2 reels per film, one film per scheduled run (9:00 and 17:00).
Quality bar: critic overall 3.5 to auto-pass, 3.0 accepted after 3 rounds, below that flagged for the owner.
Approval: while post.require_approval is true, nothing posts until the owner approves it in the dashboard.
