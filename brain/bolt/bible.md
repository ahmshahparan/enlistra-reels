# Bolt: character bible (draft for approval)

## Who he is
Bolt is a small wind-up tin robot, about the height of a coffee mug, with a brass key
turning slowly in his back. He wants to do ordinary human things well (make toast, water
a plant, catch a bus, win at chess) and is defeated, again and again, by everyday
objects. He never gives up, and once per episode he wins by accident.

## Look (keep identical in every shot)
- Body: rounded rectangular tin body, teal-blue enamel paint with small chips and two
  rivets on the chest; a single round porthole window on the chest showing gears.
- Head: a dome with one large round lens eye (warm amber light) and a tiny antenna with
  a red bead on top. No mouth.
- Limbs: short bendy arms ending in three-fingered rubber grippers; two stubby legs
  with rubber feet.
- Key: brass wind-up key in his back, always turning; it slows down when he is tired.
- Style: stylised 3D, soft clay-like shading, warm daylight, shallow depth of field,
  miniature-photography feel. Never photorealistic people; humans, if any, appear only
  as hands or legs at the frame edge.

## How he "talks"
No words. Only sounds: clockwork whirr, tin clinks, a two-note chirp for "yes", a
falling three-note boop for "oh no", a key-turning ratchet when he winds himself up.

## World
A sunny kitchen and a small garden in an apartment, seen from his height. Recurring rival: a smug robot vacuum cleaner called "the Disc" (generic design, no brand).
Recurring friend: a house sparrow who watches from the window sill.

## Episode rules
- 45 to 90 seconds, one goal, three attempts, a reversal, a tiny win or a twist ending.
- The goal is clear in the first 3 seconds (he looks at the toaster, then at the bread).
- Every episode varies the object, the place and the kind of failure.
- Ends on a single sound gag and Bolt's "oh no" or "yes" chirp.
- No violence, no scary moments, no flashing; suitable for all ages.
